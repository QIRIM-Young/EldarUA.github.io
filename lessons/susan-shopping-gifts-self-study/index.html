Write in your own words

Do these short mini-tasks independently. Write first, then reveal an example. These are open-ended: multiple good answers are possible.

Writing A · Gifts

Write 2–3 sentences recommending a gift for someone who loves reading but has a very small apartment. Include because (бо / тому що).

ExampleЯ раджу подарувати їй електронну книжку, бо вона дуже любить читати, але має мало місця вдома.I recommend giving her an e-reader because she loves reading but has little space at home.
Writing B · For whom?

Write two sentences: (1) I gave my friend a scarf. (2) I bought flowers for my mother. Use the dative in (1) and для in (2).

ExampleЯ подарувала подрузі шарф. Я купила квіти для мами.I gave my friend a scarf. I bought flowers for my mom.
Writing C · Your real life

Imagine you are choosing a thoughtful birthday gift. Describe the person, the item, and why it fits them (3 sentences). Try щоб at least once.

ExampleМій брат любить готувати. Я хочу подарувати йому нову кулінарну книжку, щоб він міг готувати цікаві страви.My brother loves cooking. I want to give him a new cookbook so that he can prepare interesting dishes.
