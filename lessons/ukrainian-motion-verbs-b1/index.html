Grammar · Part 1

1. Motion verbs without prefixes

First decide whether the movement has a particular direction or is repeated / undirected.

Зараз я йду до університету.I'm walking to the university now. (One journey in progress.)
Я ходжу до університету щодня.I go to the university every day. (Habitual trips.)
Учора я ходила до аптеки.Yesterday I went to the pharmacy (and came back). (Single round trip in the past.)
Important exception in the past: ходила / ходив can mean a single visit there and back. Compare: Я йшла до аптеки = I was on my way to the pharmacy; Я ходила до аптеки = I went to the pharmacy (the outing).

Why the future is special

One planned departure
Я піду.
I will go / set off.
Regular future
Я буду ходити.
I will go (regularly).
Grammar · Part 2

2. Nine prefixes = nine direction clues

Learn each prefix as a movement along a route. The base verb still tells you whether you travel by foot, vehicle, air, etc.

PrefixWhat it tells youExample & translation
при-arrive at a placeВін прийшов на роботу.
He arrived at work.
в- / уві-enter, move insideВона увійшла в кімнату.
She entered the room.
ви-leave an enclosed placeМи вийшли з кафе.
We came out of the café.
під- / піді-approach, move closerВін підійшов до дверей.
He walked up to the door.
від- / віді-move away fromВона відійшла від столу.
She stepped away from the table.
до- / ді-reach a destinationМи дійшли до парку.
We made it to the park.
про-pass by, go through, cover a distanceАвтобус проїхав повз школу.
The bus drove past the school.
пере-cross, go across, relocateМи перейшли через міст.
We crossed the bridge.
за-enter / stop by on the wayЯ зайшла в магазин.
I stopped by the store.
Remember the prepositions: вийти з (exit from), підійти до (approach), відійти від (move away), перейти через (cross), пройти повз (pass by), зайти в / до (stop by / enter).
Grammar · Part 3

3. Verb pairs: process vs. completed event

With prefixes, the imperfective / perfective contrast becomes very systematic.

Imperfective · Недоконаний
приходити
to be arriving / to arrive regularly
Perfective · Доконаний
прийти
to arrive (as a single completed event)

Essential pairs

Кожного ранку він приходить о восьмій.Every morning, he arrives at eight. (Repeated pattern.)
Він прийшов о восьмій.He arrived at eight. (One completed arrival.)
Word-building detail: ходити + prefix gives an imperfective pair (e.g. виходити), while іти / йти + prefix gives a perfective pair (e.g. вийти). Vehicle-motion pairs often use -їжджати / -їхати: приїжджати — приїхати. Don’t mechanically form приїхати — приїхати as the two aspect forms!
